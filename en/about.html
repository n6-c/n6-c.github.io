About

Small on purpose, and accountable for what we deliver.

N6C (Node-Six Cybernetics) makes 3D for real estate, WhatsApp assistants, websites and software, design and security reviews. Staying small is how we move fast and keep prices within reach of a small business.

Anderson Silva

Anderson has been writing software and building systems for more than 17 years. At N6C he designs each system, builds the core and answers for what we deliver.

He also brings close to a decade in information security and strategic intelligence, as a consultant and instructor, which is where our security reviews come from.

Before N6C

Anderson's previous work.

At Lucentive (remote, April to September 2026), Anderson was the lead backend engineer, working across the whole product line: Icarus, which he conceived and built, the Intuitive Agent System (IAS), Aura, Lucentive One, Lucentive Spaces, LucidMatch and Loop. Some of them are below. They were built for Lucentive, not for N6C clients.

Lucentive · 2026

Icarus

An AI platform that generates complete applications, conceived by Anderson and built with the Lucentive team. He built its core: orchestration that works with models from different providers, and build and verify gates, so whatever the models generate is checked before it's delivered. The repository history backs this up: Anderson made its first commit on 29 May 2026 and wrote 72 of its 74 commits.

Lead backend engineer · Multi-model orchestration · LLM systems · Quality gates

  • Lucentive

    Aura · Agent-to-agent client

    Lets AI agents find each other, prove who they are and talk over a live stream. Anderson built the signed discovery, the JWS and JWKS checks that refuse anything they can't verify, and JSON-RPC over SSE. The protocol checks are covered by 33 adversarial tests.

    TypeScript · JWS / JWKS · JSON-RPC · SSE

  • Lucentive

    Lucentive One · Identity

    The identity provider for Lucentive's family of apps, which Anderson designed: one sign-in across all of them with OIDC, and passwordless login with passkeys.

    OIDC · SSO · WebAuthn · Passkeys

  • Lucentive

    Lucentive Loop · Campaigns

    AI-assisted campaign production, from the brief through content generation, review and approval to social publishing. Anderson worked on the pipeline, where preview and dry-run modes keep a generated draft apart from what actually goes live.

    TypeScript · Next.js · AI orchestration

  • Other engineering

    TagSmith · macOS app

    A native editor for media metadata that never re-encodes the file. It plans each write for the format, protects the audio, video or image data and reads the result back to check it, updating in place where the container allows and rebuilding the file atomically where it doesn't.

    Swift · macOS · Binary formats

  • Other engineering

    Lua · Voice assistant

    A private voice assistant for the Mac. The language model and the text-to-speech both run on the machine itself, offline.

    Local LLM · Text-to-speech · macOS
