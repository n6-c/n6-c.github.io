01

3D for real estate

Open demo

For land developers, property developers and estate agencies. From your drawing we make the interactive sales map (the lot or unit map a buyer opens on their phone, with status, price and WhatsApp on each one), the digital scale model of the development, aerial images and rendered floor plans for listings, brochures and social media.

What you get

  • A sales map in the browser, with lot or unit status updated from your spreadsheet
  • A 3D digital scale model of the development
  • Photoreal aerial images
  • Rendered floor plans
  • Files ready for the web, social media and print

What we need from you

  • The design as DWG, DXF or PDF
  • The spreadsheet with the status of each lot or unit
  • Logo, contacts and sales rules
  • The registration number of the subdivision or development, the registry office and the CRECI of whoever advertises, for the sales map footer

Steps

  1. 01We receive the design and the spreadsheet
  2. 02Model and first image for your approval
  3. 03Changes agreed in writing
  4. 04Files delivered and the sales map published

The timeline comes in the plan, together with the price.

02

WhatsApp assistant and automations

In our own daily use

A virtual assistant on your WhatsApp or website. It introduces itself as a virtual assistant in its first message, answers the questions that keep coming up, understands voice notes and replies in a natural voice, sends floor plans and fact sheets, and hands your team, with a summary, whatever needs a person. We also automate the dull steps between the tools you already use: sorting email, filling spreadsheets, sending reports. We set it up on the official WhatsApp Business API, the route Meta provides for businesses, which lowers the risk of the number being blocked.

What you get

  • A virtual assistant set up with your company's information
  • Replies in text and voice
  • Floor plans, fact sheets and price lists sent on request
  • Hand-over to your team with a summary of the conversation
  • Monthly follow-up and adjustments

What we need from you

  • The questions you get most and the right answers
  • Price list and availability
  • A number dedicated to the service

Steps

  1. 01We gather questions, answers and rules
  2. 02We build and test it with simulated conversations
  3. 03Fine-tuning together with your team
  4. 04In operation, with monthly follow-up

The timeline comes in the plan, together with the price.

04

Technical drawing and design review

Example in demo 02

We prepare clean, standardised technical drawings (layers, dimensions, area schedules) and review designs before they go to site, to approval or to the scale model: what does not match between plan, section and site plan, overlaps, measurements that do not add up. Every point is marked on the drawing, in a PDF report.

What you get

  • Standardised drawing in DWG and PDF
  • Checked area schedule
  • Review report with the points numbered on the drawing
  • A written conversation about each point

What we need from you

  • The files as DWG, DXF or PDF
  • What the drawing is for: building, approval or sales

Steps

  1. 01We receive the files and the goal
  2. 02Cross-check of plan, section, site plan and schedule
  3. 03PDF report with the points marked
  4. 04A written conversation about each point

The timeline comes in the plan, together with the price.

Starting prices

Technical drawing and design review
On requestdepending on size and number of sheets

Starting prices, in Brazilian reais. The final price depends on the size of the job and comes in the written plan, before we start.

The review is not an expert report or a formal technical opinion, and neither the review nor the drawings carry an ART (Brazil's record of technical responsibility): the professional who signs the design remains responsible for it.

05

Security review

Only with written permission

With your written permission, we check whether your company's email domain can be used to send fake messages, look for common weaknesses in your website or web app, and gather what anyone can find about your company online. You get a plain-language report with what to fix first. It is a review, not a full penetration test.

What you get

  • A plain-language report, with priorities
  • The fixes, step by step, for whoever looks after your website and email
  • A written conversation about each point

What we need from you

  • Written permission from the person responsible for the company
  • The domains, websites and systems in scope

Steps

  1. 01Permission and scope in writing
  2. 02Checks within the agreed scope
  3. 03Report with what to fix first

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

Each service has starting prices on this page. The final price depends on the size of the job and comes in writing, in a short plan, before we start. Half up front, half on delivery, by PIX in Brazil. The smallest job we take is R$ 1,000.

How long does it take?

The timeline comes in the plan, together with the price, and is agreed before we start.

Do I need to understand technology?

No. Tell us what happens today and what you would like to solve. The technical part is ours.

Do you use artificial intelligence?

Yes, at several stages of the work, including the first reply on our WhatsApp, which comes from our virtual assistant. Every project has Anderson as the accountable lead, and everything we deliver is tested and checked before it reaches you.

Do you work outside Brazil?

Yes. We work in English and Portuguese, and clients outside Brazil pay in US dollars, with the amount fixed in the plan. Node-Six Cybernetics is based in Tartu, Estonia.

Tell us what is costing you time or money.

A few lines are enough. We reply in writing with what we would build and what it costs, in English or Portuguese.
