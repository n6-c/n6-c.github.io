Design review

Before modelling or building, we check plan, section, site plan and area schedule against each other. Every point is numbered on the drawing and explained in a PDF report, with what to check and with whom.

What the report contains

  • The points marked and numbered on the drawing
  • For each point: what does not match, where, and why it matters
  • Priority: critical, attention or note
  • What was checked and is right, too

How we work

No drawn-out meetings. Everything in writing.

  1. 01

    Tell us the problem

    On WhatsApp or by email, in a few lines, as text or a voice note. Send what you already have: CAD, PDF, spreadsheet, photos. No technical knowledge needed.

  2. 02

    A fixed price before we start

    You get a short written plan and a single price. Half up front, half on delivery. If the scope changes, the change is agreed in writing first.

  3. 03

    Delivery and follow-up

    Delivered, tested and explained in writing. Afterwards, if you like, we look after it for a monthly fee, and you always know whom to write to.
