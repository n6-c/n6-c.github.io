What we do

Four things we do well.

Not sure which one you need? Describe the problem and we'll tell you honestly whether we can help.

WhatsApp assistants and automations

An assistant on your WhatsApp or website that answers the common questions, understands voice notes, takes files and passes the rest to someone on your team. Behind it, automations for the dull steps between the tools you already use: sorting email, filling sheets, sending reports.

Custom software and dashboards

Internal tools, client portals, websites, Android and Mac apps. Dashboards that turn your spreadsheets or ERP exports into something you can read at a glance, updated on their own.

3D site models and showcases

For developers and architects. We turn your CAD or DWG drawings into a 3D model of the site with renders, or into an interactive showcase buyers can walk through in the browser.

Written security reviews

With your written permission, we check whether your email domain can be spoofed, test your website or web app for the common weaknesses, and look at what anyone can find about your company online. You get a plain-language report with the fixes. It's a review, not a certified pentest.

Anderson's previous work.

IcarusLucentive · 2026

An AI platform that generates complete applications. The idea and the build are Anderson's, in partnership with the Lucentive team. Aura · Agent-to-agent client

Lets AI agents find each other, prove who they are and talk over a live stream. Anderson built the signed discovery, the JWS and JWKS checks that refuse anything they can't verify, and JSON-RPC over SSE. The protocol checks are covered by 33 adversarial tests.
